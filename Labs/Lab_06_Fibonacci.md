### Code a program that displays the first 10 terms of the Fibonacci sequence.

**What is the Fibonacci Sequence?**

1, 1, 2, 3, 5, etc.

Note that the first two terms of the Fibonacci numbers are 1 and 1, and every other term in the Fibonacci sequence is the sum of the previous two terms.

**Hint:** Use three different variables, `f1` and `f2`, to represent the previous two terms in the Fibonacci sequence, and `current` to represent the current term in the Fibonacci sequence.  Update all three variables within your loop.
